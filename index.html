How to play

Three simple steps to sweetness

Grab your phone, look through the viewfinder, and snap photos of the colors hiding all around you.

1

Pick a mission

Choose a mission from the Kitchen — like Sunrise Drive, Ocean Blue, or Rainbow Road.

2

Point & snap

Point your camera at anything colorful — a red apple, a yellow taxi, a blue sky. Tap the donut shutter (or the volume buttons!).

3

Collect donuts

Match the color donuts to complete the mission and unlock the next one. Fill your Color Dex one photo at a time!

Privacy

Your privacy is baked in

Donut Hunt is a single-player, offline game. Your photos stay on your device — always.

📵

100% Offline

The game never connects to the internet. No data is ever transmitted to any server.

🚫

No Data Collected

No personal data, device info, usage stats, or location. No analytics, no ads SDKs, nothing.

🔒

Photos Stay On-Device

Color analysis happens entirely on your phone. Photos are never uploaded, stored, or shared.
